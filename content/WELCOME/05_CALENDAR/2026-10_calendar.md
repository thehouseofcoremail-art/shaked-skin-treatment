# October 2026 — Welcome content calendar

**Goals, in priority order:** brand exposure/awareness → engagement (comments, shares,
saves) → follower growth → sales.
**Tone:** authentic, not salesy, professional but light, young/current.
**Governing constraint:** `content/WELCOME/00_BRAND/BRAND_BRIEF.md` is entirely blank
and the feature/pricing list is unverified (`03_MEDIA/TEMPLATE_LIBRARY.md`, open since
2026-08-27). Per the operating rule "missing fact → ask, never invent," this calendar
is built almost entirely from pillars that need no product fact: **inspiration,
aesthetic, relatable friction/humor, guest-experience dramatization, and engagement
bait.** The one slot that would naturally call for a verified-feature or pricing post
is marked **BLOCKED** below rather than filled with an invented claim.

Full research backing this plan: `04_RESEARCH/2026-09-14_october-calendar-context.md`.
Full frame-by-frame creative for Week 1 (both feed posts, its six story days, and its
Reel): `06_DRAFTS/W-02_2026-10-week1-full-detail.md`.
